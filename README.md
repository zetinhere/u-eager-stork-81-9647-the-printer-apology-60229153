One funny one-line apology from an office printer to the people who keep jamming it, feeding it cheap paper and swearing at it. Twelve candidate lines were drafted against the judge's brief (genuine apology with the printer's own gripes, a real joke with the punch at the end, printer truth, freshness, economy inside 120 characters), scored on those criteria, and the program picks the highest-scoring one and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

The line it writes:

> Sorry I jammed on your deadline. After a decade of bargain paper and a staple in my throat, I call it self-defence.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
